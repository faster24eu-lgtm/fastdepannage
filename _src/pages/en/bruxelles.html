Towing in Brussels is different

The city has its pitfalls, and knowing them saves everyone time:

Underground car parks

Many apartment blocks and shopping centres have an entrance lower than 2 m. Give us the maximum height shown at the entrance and the level where the car is. If the tow truck can't get down, we first bring the car up.

The tunnels and the Small Ring

Breaking down in a Brussels tunnel is dangerous. Stay in the car with your seatbelt on, hazard lights on, and call 112 or use the emergency phone in the tunnel. The tunnels are monitored: the operator has the car removed first. Then call us for the rest of the trip.

Narrow streets and parking

In Saint-Gilles, the Marolles or upper Ixelles there is little space. The tow truck needs some room behind the car to load it. We sort it out together with you, and if need be we push the car to a wider spot.

The low emission zone (LEZ)

A car on a flatbed isn't being driven: the LEZ is no obstacle to towing it, even if it's no longer allowed to drive in Brussels. Useful for taking it to a garage, a buyer or outside the Region.

Questions about towing in Brussels

How quickly can you be there in Brussels?

That depends on the time and the municipality: the Ring at 8 am is not the Ring at 11 pm. We give you a realistic arrival time on the phone, and let you know if it changes.

Can you take my car to a garage outside Brussels?

Yes, in Flanders, in Wallonia or further. We agree the price for the whole trip before we set off.

My car has had an accident. Can you wait for the police?

Yes. If the police need to make their report, we only load the car once they agree. Also fill in the accident report form with the other driver.
