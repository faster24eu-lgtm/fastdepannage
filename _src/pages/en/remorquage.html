Your car towed on a flatbed

Engine trouble, an accident, a gearbox that's stuck or simply a car that won't drive any more: we load it onto our flatbed and take it wherever you want, in Brussels, in Flanders or further.

Why a flatbed?

A flatbed carries the whole car. No wheel touches the road and nothing turns in the gearbox or the drivetrain. That is the only safe way to move an automatic, a four-wheel drive, an electric or hybrid car, or a car with a locked steering wheel.

We strap down each wheel separately, not the bodywork. Your car arrives the way it left.

We tow, among others

  • Cars with engine, clutch or gearbox problems
  • Cars after an accident, driveable or not
  • Electric and hybrid cars
  • Low cars, sports cars and classic cars
  • Vans up to 3.5 tonnes (see vans)
  • Cars in a car park or on private land, with the owner's agreement

Questions about towing

Can I ride along in the tow truck?

Usually yes, in the cab, depending on the number of free seats. Tell us on the phone how many of you there are, so we can arrange something if there are more than two.

My car is in an underground car park. Is that possible?

Often yes, but the height at the entrance decides how we do it. Give us the maximum height shown at the entrance and the level. If the tow truck can't get in, we first bring the car up to the ramp.

Who pays for towing after an accident?

Depending on the situation, your insurance or the other driver's. We give you a clear invoice to send on. Fill in the accident report form on the spot, before the car leaves.

Do you tow electric cars?

Yes, always on a flatbed and without the driven wheels turning. Put the car in transport mode if the manufacturer provides one, or we do it together with you.
