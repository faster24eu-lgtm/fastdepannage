Our promises on price

  • The agreed price is the price you pay, as long as the job matches what you described.
  • If the situation changes on the spot (for example, the car won't start with the booster and has to be loaded after all), you get the new price first, before we go on.
  • An invoice for every job, for your insurer or your employer.

Beware of prices on the phone that are too low. Some middlemen quote a low price to win the job, then charge "call-out", "straps" or a "night supplement" on the spot. Always ask: "Is that the total price, everything included, all the way to the garage?" With us the answer is yes.

Questions about price

How do I find cheap breakdown recovery?

The cheapest option is often not towing at all: a battery or a tyre we fix on the spot. Then compare prices "all-inclusive to the destination", never a starting price. And choose a nearby garage if your usual one is far away: every loaded kilometre counts.

Will my breakdown cover (VAB, Touring, insurance) pay me back?

That depends on your contract. Some reimburse a tow you arranged yourself on presentation of the invoice, others only if they arranged it. Check your terms or call your breakdown service. You always get a detailed invoice from us.

Is towing more expensive in Brussels than in Flanders?

Not because it's Brussels. What counts is the distance to you and to the destination, and the time spent in traffic.
