Ghent, a city of its own for cars

The traffic circulation plan

The centre of Ghent is divided into sectors: you can't drive from one sector to another, you first have to go back to the R40 ring road. To reach you quickly, the exact address or your exact location matters even more here than elsewhere.

The pedestrian zone and the LEZ

The historic centre is car-free and Ghent has its own low emission zone. If your car is in a street with restricted access, tell us: we arrange access or come to the nearest point.

The E40 and the E17

Both motorways cross at Zwijnaarde, one of the busiest interchanges in Flanders. As everywhere on Flemish motorways, if the car is blocking traffic, the FAST tow truck comes first. See motorway breakdown.
