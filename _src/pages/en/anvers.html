What you need to know in Antwerp

The R1 Ring and the tunnels

The Antwerp Ring is one of the busiest stretches in the country, and the Kennedy and Liefkenshoek tunnels have their own rules: a broken-down car is removed very quickly by the assigned service. Stay in the car, hazard lights on, and wait for instructions. As soon as the car is out of the tunnel, we take over.

The port

The port areas are huge and addresses there can be vague. Give us the quay number or the name of the nearest company.

The low emission zone

Antwerp has its own LEZ. A car on a flatbed isn't being driven, so we can take it out of the city, even if it's no longer allowed to drive there.
