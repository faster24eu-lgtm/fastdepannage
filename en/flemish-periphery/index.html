The Ring, the airport and the business parks

Most of our call-outs around Brussels happen in three kinds of places:

On the R0 Ring

The Zaventem, Groot-Bijgaarden and Drogenbos interchanges handle a huge amount of traffic. If your car is in the way on the Ring, the police can call in the assigned tow company of the FAST system. Afterwards, we collect the car where it was left. It's all explained on the motorway breakdown page.

In the airport car parks

Back from holiday, flat battery after two weeks in long-stay parking: a classic. Give us the name of the car park and the zone. For closed car parks the operator's agreement is sometimes needed: we tell you what to ask.

In the business parks

Vilvoorde, Machelen, Diegem, Groot-Bijgaarden, Zellik: company vans that have broken down, lease cars that need to go to the dealer. We invoice in the company's name.

Frequently asked questions

My car has broken down in the airport car park. Can you come?

In most car parks, yes. Give us the name of the car park and the zone. For a starting problem we come with a booster; for a car that won't start at all, we first check whether the tow truck can get in.

My garage in Brussels only speaks French or Dutch. Is that a problem?

Not at all. We speak English, French and Dutch, and handle the conversation with the mechanic.

Broken down right now?

Call us now. You get a fixed price and an arrival time, in English, before we set off.
