Broken down on the Ring or the motorway

The Ring around Brussels lies almost entirely in Flanders, and different towing rules apply there than in the city. This is what happens, and this is what you can do yourself.

Call 03 375 67 37

Your safety first

  1. If you can still drive, carry on to the next exit or car park. A hard shoulder is never a safe place.
  2. Hazard lights on, high-visibility vest on before you open the door.
  3. Everyone gets out on the passenger side and stands behind the crash barrier.
  4. Warning triangle at least 100 m behind the car, only if it's safe to do so.
  5. If the car is blocking a lane or anyone is injured: call 112.

Where exactly are you? Look at the small white posts along the crash barrier: they show the road number and the kilometre. With your direction of travel (towards Liège, towards Ghent…) we find you straight away.

The FAST system on Flemish motorways

On the motorways and the Ring in Flanders, the authorities' first aim is to clear the road. If a broken-down or damaged car is blocking traffic, the police or the Traffic Centre (Verkeerscentrum) can call in a tow truck from the FAST system (Files Aanpakken door Snelle Tussenkomst, "tackling traffic jams through rapid intervention"). That tow company is assigned to that stretch of motorway: you don't choose it yourself, and you pay the costs if you are not covered by breakdown assistance.

Its job is to move the car to a safe place, not necessarily to your garage. That's where we come in: we collect the car where it was left and take it wherever you want.

If your car is safe and not in anyone's way (in a car park, at the exit, at a car-sharing car park), you can call us directly.

On the motorways in the Brussels Region

In Brussels itself (the A12 into the centre, the E40 up to Reyers, the tunnels of the Small Ring) there is no similar system. If you are stuck in a tunnel, don't walk into the traffic: switch on your hazard lights and use the emergency phones or call 112. The tunnels are monitored by cameras.

Frequently asked questions

Can I refuse the FAST tow truck and wait for my own tow company?

If your car is blocking traffic on a Flemish motorway, usually not: the police decide on removing it. Once the car is in a safe place, you can call us for the rest.

How much does a tow on the Ring cost?

For our job you get a fixed price on the phone, depending on the location and the destination. A FAST intervention is charged by the assigned tow company, at its own rates.

My car has been towed away and I don't know where it is.

Contact the police (101) or the Traffic Centre: they know which tow company intervened and where the car is. Then call us to collect it.

Broken down right now?

Call us now. You get a fixed price and an arrival time, in English, before we set off.
