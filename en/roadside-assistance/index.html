Roadside assistance on the spot, without towing

Many breakdowns don't need a garage. A flat battery or a flat tyre we often fix where you are, and you drive on yourself.

Call 03 375 67 37

Flat battery

The classic in winter and with cars that stand on the street for a long time. We start the car with a booster and check whether the alternator is charging. If the battery is worn out, we tell you honestly: otherwise it will let you down again tomorrow morning.

Good to know: after starting, drive for at least twenty minutes, preferably outside the city, without switching off the engine.

Flat tyre

We fit your spare wheel or space-saver. No spare wheel, only a repair kit that won't do the job (damaged sidewall, torn tyre)? Then we load the car and take it to a tyre centre.

Good to know: check whether the locking wheel nut adapter is in the glovebox. Without it we can't get the wheel off.

Out of petrol or diesel

It happens to the best of us, especially when the gauge lies. We get you to the nearest filling station, on the flatbed if need be.

Wrong fuel (petrol in a diesel or the other way round)? Do not start the engine, and don't even switch on the ignition. We tow the car to a garage that drains the tank.

Keys locked in or lost

Keys in the locked car: give us the make and model, and we tell you on the phone whether we can open it on the spot without damage. Keys lost: then the car has to go to the dealer or a key specialist, and we take it there.

And if it can't be fixed on the spot?

Then we load the car. The vehicle that comes out for roadside assistance is a flatbed: no second truck has to come, and you don't have to wait again.

On the phone we tell you in advance what the roadside repair costs and what towing would cost if it's needed. You know where you stand.

Frequently asked questions

An engine warning light is on. Can that be fixed on the spot?

Sometimes. With an orange light you can often drive calmly to the garage. A red light (oil, temperature, brakes) means: stop and don't drive any further. Describe the light to us and we'll tell you what's sensible.

Do you sell batteries?

We start the car and give advice, but a new battery is best fitted by a garage or car centre, which gives you the right part and a guarantee.

My car is in a narrow street in Brussels. Can you get there?

For a roadside repair, almost always. To load the car, the tow truck needs some space behind it. If the street really is too narrow, we find a solution together.

Broken down right now?

Call us now. You get a fixed price and an arrival time, in English, before we set off.
