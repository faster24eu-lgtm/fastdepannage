First things first: get yourself safe

The car can wait. You can't. It only takes a few seconds:

  • Switch on your hazard lights and put on your high-visibility vest before you get out.
  • Get out on the side away from the traffic and make everyone get out.
  • Place the warning triangle at least 30 m behind the car, 100 m on the motorway.
  • On the motorway, wait behind the crash barrier, never in front of or inside the car.
  • If anyone is injured or the car is blocking a lane: call 112 first.

Then call us and tell us where you are. On the motorway, also give the number on the marker post next to the crash barrier and your direction of travel.

Frequently asked questions

How much does a tow cost in Brussels?

The price depends on where you are, where the car needs to go, the time of day and the type of vehicle. You get a fixed price on the phone, before the tow truck sets off. Our prices page explains what determines the amount.

How quickly can you be there?

That depends on the location and the traffic. We give you a realistic arrival time on the phone, instead of promising "15 minutes anywhere", which no tow company in Brussels can keep at rush hour.

I have breakdown cover (VAB, Touring or similar). Can I still call you?

Yes. Your breakdown service remains the first step if your contract covers it. If the wait is too long, you are not covered or you want to choose your own garage, you can call us directly. Keep the invoice: some contracts reimburse on presentation.

How do I find a tow truck near me right now?

Call us and tell us where you are. You immediately get an arrival time and a price, and you decide.

Do you speak English?

Yes. We work in English, French and Dutch. If your garage speaks French or Dutch, we handle the conversation with the mechanic for you.

Where do you take the car?

Wherever you want: your usual garage, the brand dealer, your home or a secure car park if the garage is closed at night. For long distances we agree the price on the phone.
