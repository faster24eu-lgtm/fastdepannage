Broken down on holiday: what you're wondering

How do we get home?

Depending on the free seats in the cab, you can ride along with the car. If you're with your family, tell us straight away on the phone so we can find the right solution. The train from the coast to Brussels is also an easy option.

Repair it here or take it home?

A battery or a tyre we often fix on the spot. For a real breakdown you are usually better off going to your own mechanic, rather than leaving the car at an unknown garage 120 km from home. You get the price of both options.

The hotel or apartment car park

Many car parks at the coast are underground and low. Give us the maximum height shown at the entrance.
