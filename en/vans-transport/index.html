Vans & vehicle transport

A broken-down van often means a lost working day. And moving a car without driving it is something you plan. We do both.

Call 03 375 67 79

Vans up to 3.5 tonnes

Transit, Sprinter, Crafter, Master, Jumper, Ducato: our flatbeds carry the common vans, long wheelbase included. Give us the model, the length and whether it's loaded. A van full of equipment soon weighs more than you think, and we'd rather know before we set off.

  • Towing to your garage or the dealer
  • Drop-off at your home or your company's depot
  • Invoice in your company's name

A lorry over 3.5 tonnes? Call us anyway: we'll tell you honestly whether we can take it or who you'd better call.

Planned transport of your car

Not an emergency, but a car that needs to get from A to B without being driven:

  • A car bought from a private seller or a garage that needs to go home
  • A car without a valid roadworthiness test, to the test centre or the garage
  • A classic car or a low car, on a flatbed
  • Repatriation after a breakdown abroad: France, Germany, the Netherlands and further

Together we agree the day, the time and an all-inclusive price.

Call us now. You get a fixed price and an arrival time, in English, before we set off.
