Car towed away in Brussels: what now?

You come back to where you parked, and the car has gone. Before you think of theft: there is a good chance it has been towed away. This is how to find it and get it back.

1. Stolen or towed away?

Look around: a sign for a house move, roadworks, a market, an event, a garage entrance, a reserved space. In Brussels, a car that is in the way is towed to a pound on police orders. Usually no note is left behind.

Call the local police of the municipality where you parked, or 101 (not 112, it's not an emergency). With your number plate they can tell you whether the car was towed and where it is. If it wasn't towed, report it stolen at that point.

2. Getting permission to collect it

The pound won't simply hand the car over. Usually you first have to go to the police station of the police zone that had the car towed, which gives you a release document. Ask on the phone for the opening hours and which documents to bring. In practice:

  • Your identity card or passport
  • The car's registration certificate
  • The insurance green card
  • If the car isn't in your name (company car, lease, a relative's car): a power of attorney from the owner

3. Paying and getting the car out of the pound

At the pound you pay the towing costs and the storage costs, which go up every day. So don't wait too long. Ask which payment methods are accepted before you go.

Walk round the car before you sign anything, and note any damage.

4. And if the car can't be driven?

That's where we can help. A flat battery after a few days in the pound, an expired roadworthiness test, a car that is no longer allowed in the low emission zone, no valid insurance: in all these cases we load it onto our flatbed at the pound and take it home or to your garage.

Call us with the address of the pound and your destination as soon as you have the release document. You get a fixed price and we meet you at the pound.

How to avoid it next time

  • Temporary signs (house moves, roadworks) are often put up a few days in advance: a quick look is enough when you park the car for several days.
  • On market days, parking is banned early in the morning in the streets concerned.
  • A car left for a long time in front of a garage entrance or in a reserved space can be towed away at the request of the police.

Frequently asked questions

How much does it cost if my car has been towed away in Brussels?

The towing and storage costs depend on the municipality and the pound, and the storage costs go up every day. Ask the police or the pound for the exact amount when you call them.

Can you collect the car from the pound for me?

The release document is given to the owner or a person with a power of attorney, and the pound has to be paid. The simplest way is to go to the pound together with us: you handle the papers, we handle the car.

My roadworthiness test has expired. Can I still drive?

Better not take that risk. On a flatbed the car isn't being driven: that's the safe way to get it to the garage or the test centre.
