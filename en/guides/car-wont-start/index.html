Practical tip

My car won't start: what now?

Before you call anyone: listen to what the car does when you turn the key or press the button. The sound (or the silence) often tells you everything.

Nothing, or a "click"

Dashboard dead or dim, one dry click or a series of clicks: it is almost always the battery. Lights left on, cold weather, a car that stood still for a long time, or a worn-out battery.

On the spot: a jump start with a booster solves it in most cases.

The engine turns over but won't fire

The starter turns the engine, but it doesn't catch. Often: an empty tank (the gauge can lie), the fuel pump, or the wrong fuel if you've just filled up.

Careful: if you've just put petrol in a diesel (or the other way round), don't keep trying.

Key or alarm light flashing

The immobiliser doesn't recognise the key. Try the spare key, replace the key battery, or hold the key against the start button (many cars have an emergency reader there).

A loud "clunk" and then nothing

Battery fine, lights stay bright, but one dry clunk and the engine doesn't turn: possibly the starter motor. That can't be fixed at the roadside: the car has to go to the garage.

What you can check yourself

  • Automatic: lever firmly in P, foot on the brake.
  • Steering wheel locked: turn it slightly while you start to release the steering lock.
  • Fuel gauge: if it was in the red, look no further.
  • Interior light or headlights left on all night: then it's the battery.
  • Error message or red warning light on the dashboard: note what it says, that helps us.

When to call us

If the car still won't start after these checks, call us and tell us where you are, which car it is and what happens when you try to start it. We'll tell you whether a start on the spot has a chance, or whether the car is better loaded straight away, with the price of each option.
